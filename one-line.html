CareLine Concierge is a 24/7 resident and family concierge service designed specifically for nursing homes. We provide dedicated support, communication, and assistance from admission through discharge: a real person families can reach for everyday requests and for the moments that can't wait.

And because every call and text becomes insight for the facility, leadership finally sees what residents and families are experiencing, in real time.

What we do

More than a phone line.

Residents and families get a single number to call or text. Your teams get concerns that arrive documented, routed, and tracked to resolution.

  1. One number, any hour

    Residents and families reach a real person, day or night, for everyday requests and time-sensitive matters alike.

  2. Heard, then documented

    Every concern is captured in full and logged to the resident's record, so nothing lives in a hallway conversation.

  3. Routed and followed through

    We send each matter to the right team and stay on it until it's addressed, then close the loop with the family.

  4. Admission through discharge

    We support both ends of the stay, gather resident and family feedback, and hand leadership organized reporting they can act on.
