    Our promise

    Let us handle the communication, while you earn our trust.

    CareLine Concierge is a 24/7 resident and family concierge service designed specifically for nursing homes, providing dedicated support, communication, and assistance from admission through discharge.

    Residents and their families get a dedicated point of contact they can call or text for any need, from everyday requests to time-sensitive matters. We make sure each concern is heard, properly documented, communicated to the appropriate team, and followed through until it is addressed.

    But CareLine is more than a communication service. Every call and text becomes valuable insight for the facility: real-time visibility for owners, administrators, and leadership into what residents and families are actually experiencing.

    What we do

    More than a phone line.
